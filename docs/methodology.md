# Prioritization Methodology

This project evaluates requisitions across eight planning dimensions:

1. Business impact
2. Time sensitivity
3. Headcount approval
4. Pipeline health
5. Hiring risk
6. Estimated recruiting effort
7. Requisition age
8. Dependencies affecting teams or commitments

## Example weights

| Dimension | Example weight |
|---|---:|
| Business impact | 25% |
| Time sensitivity | 20% |
| Headcount approval | 20% |
| Pipeline risk | 15% |
| Requisition age | 10% |
| Recruiting effort | 10% |

These weights are discussion aids, not objective facts. Organizations should adapt them with recruiting and business leadership.

## Human review requirements

- Verify all supplied facts.
- Never infer priority from stakeholder seniority or escalation frequency.
- Never include candidate names or personal information.
- Document human adjustments.
- Revisit the recommendation when circumstances change.
- Do not use this tool to select, reject, score, or rank candidates.
